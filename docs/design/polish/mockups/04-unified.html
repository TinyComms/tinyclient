Polish 04 · Inbox, People, rail, quick switcher

Spec: docs/design/polish/04-unified.md. Frames are 1120 × 720 pt, dark, at the default widths (rail 72, sidebar 316). All names are invented. Only fills, 1 pt borders, radii, shadows and SVG are used, all of which GPUI can draw.

1 · Inbox, four networks signed in

Pinned, then day groups. Monochrome network mark leads each title because four networks are in. Channel rows use the Space's icon; the Space name trails in faint and gives way first. Line 2 shows typing, sender, photos, failed send, draft and missed call. Filled blue = mentions you; outlined count = unread DM. Muted Priya is dimmed with a bell. Bots fold into one Apps row (dot, no count). Rail: one number (7 = 6 unread DMs/groups + 1 mention).

7 · Rail, every badge state

One number, on Inbox only: outlined when it is only unread, blue when a mention is inside. Network buttons and pins get a dot: blue for a mention, white for unread, nothing when muted. Selection is the 44 pt tile fill and a brighter glyph; there is no bar. An expired account shows at 40% and its home shows a banner. Pins end in a scroll; a hairline marks the cut. The own menu sets presence for each account or for all.

Network people-search runs after a pause and shows one line of words while it waits; results join the People group below the selected row, never above it.

10 · Before and after: the Inbox

Left: `tinyclient --shot` of pre-polish with the demo seed (Discord and Slack). Right: frame 1's rail and sidebar.

Before (pre-polish) → After

13 full-colour logos → monochrome marks, and only when two or more networks are in.

“Moss… › #showcase”, “H… › Mara” → the name is never cut; the Space trails in faint and is dropped before it shrinks below 6 characters.

One undivided run → Pinned / Today / Yesterday / This week / Earlier, ending at 30 days.

@1 and 3 drawn as the same pill → filled blue numeral for a mention, outlined numeral for unread.

“@Robin Vale …” in previews → the leading mention of me is removed; the sender is shown instead.

Rail 32 / 22 / 10 → one number on Inbox, dots on networks.

Bots among people → one Apps row with a dot.

Read channel rows vanish → kept for 24 h after the mention, in place.
