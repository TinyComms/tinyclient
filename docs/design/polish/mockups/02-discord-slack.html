300 channels, hide-muted honored

Over 80 visible channels: every category without a number starts collapsed. A collapsed category keeps the open row, unread unmuted channels, rows with a number and voice channels with people. Muted categories start collapsed; muted channels are hidden because the user set Hide muted channels in Discord.

Result: 10 headers + 9 rows that need you. Two footers: Show 41 muted channels (this session) and Browse 212 more channels (onboarding opt-out, from the stored tree).

The user’s own expand and collapse wins after first open and is kept per server.

Arithmetic

300 channels × 28 = 8,400 pt as a flat list. Collapsed: 21 rows = 588 pt, one screen (the list has 624).

What changed

  • DMs are two-line rows with presence and what the person is doing; 10, not 6.
  • Friends, requests and In voice at the top: who is around is visible before any server.
  • Server rows lose the chevron; one click opens the tree.
  • Folders come back, collapsed, still showing what needs you.
  • One mention look (accent numeral, no “@”); muted is faint with no bell.
  • A section label over Servers, aligned with Direct messages.
