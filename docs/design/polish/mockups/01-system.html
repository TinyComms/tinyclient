Polish 01 · The system, consistency and app chrome

The frames the other six designers draw against. Sizes are points at 1×. Every name is invented. Spec: docs/design/polish/01-system.md. No blur, no filters, no blend modes: only fills, borders, radii and drop shadows.

Items that do not apply are disabled, never hidden; names follow the open place ("Mark Workspace as Read", "Open in Slack"). macOS adds Dictation, AutoFill and the tiling items itself.
