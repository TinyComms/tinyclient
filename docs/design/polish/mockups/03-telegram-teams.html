Polish 03 · The Telegram home and the Teams home

Spec: docs/design/polish/03-telegram-teams.md. Dark theme, tokens from 01-system. 48 rows for faces with last lines, 28 rows for places and folds. Telegram's folders are filters, so tabs; Teams' sections are where a chat lives, so headers. All names are invented.

A · Telegram · All, with the Archive row

Folder tabs (All has no count; Work holds a mention, so its count is accent). Archive is one 28 row above the Pinned header; Pinned and Chats are headers, not glyphs or rules. Shape says person (circle) or place (rounded square). One mark at most: verified, bot, Scam, forum glyph. Rows show: selected + mention, typing, read ✓✓ leading the preview, unread channel (no sender, a dot), forum with "Topic › Sender", service chat, draft, bot, muted (all faint, no glyph, no count), failed send, scam.
