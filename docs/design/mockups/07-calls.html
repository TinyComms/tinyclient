07 · Calls

One call dock at the bottom of the sidebar carries every call state on every network: ringing out, ringing in, connecting, in a call, failed. A voice call is 96 px of bar and nothing else. Video is one surface, and only when asked for. Flat fills, 1 px borders, box shadows on popovers only; no blur, nothing animated but a ring that switches on and off. Spec: docs/design/07-calls.md.

1Conversation header: native call and hand-off

Same 30 px pill, same place. A native call is a filled pill with a phone or speaker glyph. A hand-off is an outlined pill that carries the network's own icon and an out-arrow, and its label always names the destination. A conversation with no call capability has no button.
